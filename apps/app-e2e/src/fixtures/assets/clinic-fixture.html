About the clinic

Northside Aesthetics Clinic is a doctor-led skin and aesthetics clinic on Talbot Street in Dublin 1. Our patients are mostly professionals aged 30–55 who want natural, understated results from a medically qualified team. Every treatment begins with a free consultation, and every injectable is administered by a registered doctor or nurse prescriber. We are fully insured, CQC-equivalent registered with the Irish Medical Council, and have over 600 five-star reviews.
